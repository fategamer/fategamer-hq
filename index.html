The story

From 33, Hola.

Before the name FATE GAMER, there was a young man from 33, Hola, Kenya, with big dreams and very little certainty about how far those dreams could go.

Hola is more than a pin on a map. It is where the journey began — and where the refusal to stay small was born.

Life does not always give you the cards you want. You still have to play.

The music moves between Sheng, Kiswahili and English because the artist refuses one box. Some records make you move. Some make you think. All of them come from a life still being written.

Away from the microphone he builds systems — procurement, supply chain, ventures. Creativity, execution and discipline are the same person, not competing identities.

This is not a story told from the finish line. Songs are still waiting. Stages are still waiting. The ambition is global. The start was local.
